## 📋 About the Project

Kenafaim is a comprehensive attendance management application designed specifically for **Kenafaim Arts Organization** - a multi-disciplinary arts center that brings together creators who use art as a way of life and a means of expression and processing mental health challenges.

The app serves **150+ users** across three main user groups:
- **120 Artists** - Creative participants in various art programs
- **16 Mentors** - Art instructors and guides
- **6 Staff Members** - Administrative and operational team

### 🎯 Problem We Solve

Before Kenafaim app, the organization faced:
- **Multiple communication channels** with no integration
- **Scattered information** across different platforms
- **Manual attendance tracking** leading to data loss
- **Inefficient coordination** between artists, mentors, and staff

### 💡 Our Solution

A unified digital platform that:
- **Centralizes all communication** in one application
- **Streamlines attendance tracking** with real-time updates
- **Improves coordination** between all stakeholders
- **Provides detailed analytics** for better decision-making
- **Enhances user experience** with role-based interfaces

## 🏗️ System Architecture

### Core Systems
- **Authentication System** - Secure login and user management
- **Lesson Management** - Create, edit, and manage art lessons
- **Booking System** - Room reservation and scheduling
- **Attendance Tracking** - Real-time attendance monitoring
- **Reporting & Analytics** - Comprehensive data insights
- **Messaging System** - Internal communication platform
- **Notification System** - Automated alerts and reminders
- **Working Hours** - Time tracking and management

### Art Studios Supported
- 🎨 **Plastic Arts**
- 🎭 **Theater**
- ✍️ **Creative Writing**
- 🎵 **Music**
- 🎬 **Animation & Video**
- 🎪 **Performance Arts**
